Language proficiency and safety — one and the same

Safety in aviation means accuracy and good use of language. Clear communication is a requirement for flight crew and Air Traffic Controllers, who must show a minimum acceptable level of English through a proficiency test.

The test is a standardised oral assessment measuring speaking ability in the context of aviation operations, and the ability to listen to pilot / ATC exchanges in live interaction.

Assessed against the six ICAO criteria

Pronunciation Structure Vocabulary Fluency Comprehension Interactions

Proficiency levels & retesting

Level 6 — ExpertRetesting not required
Level 5 — ExtendedEvery six years
Level 4 — Operational (minimum pass)Every three years
Levels 1–3Licence not issued / maintained

A Level 4 requires meeting all six criteria at Level 4 — effective voice-only and face-to-face communication, clarity on work-related topics, and handling unexpected situations.

The test: a 20–25 minute interview

With a Subject Matter Expert (a pilot) plus a language expert assessing:

  • Your experience and interest in aviation
  • Questions on aviation operations and procedures
  • Describing an emergency-situation picture and speculating on causes
  • A role-play
  • Listening to radio exchanges and answering questions

How to prepare

General English courses plus focused test preparation. Candidates at Upper-Intermediate level in IH English courses can prepare themselves for the test.
