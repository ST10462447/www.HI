Teach it — Teacher Training

CELTA — Cambridge Certificate in English Language Teaching to Adults

Full-time initial qualification, typically 4 weeks, awarded by the University of Cambridge. For candidates with little or no teaching experience.

Course description

Introductory teacher training for candidates with little or no previous TEFL experience. Recognised by EFL organisations worldwide — the most highly respected certificate of its kind.

Applicant profile

Awareness of language and competence in written and spoken English; education standard allowing entry to Higher Education; usually 20+ years old.

Course requirements

Min 120 hours over 4 weeks: input, tutorials, supervised lesson planning, 6 hours assessed teaching practice, peer observation, 6 hours directed observation, plus min 80 hours unsupervised work.

Assessment

Awarded on meeting criteria for all written and practical assignments: needs assessment, lesson planning, language knowledge, teaching skills, professional awareness.

Teaching practice

Daily practice in groups of six; teach at least two different levels; verbal and written tutor feedback on each assessed lesson.
